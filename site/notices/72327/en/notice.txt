Title:
HPE Product Security Update Advisory

Summary:
We are disseminating today's KISA announcement "HPE Product Security Update Advisory". We recommend that representatives at each affiliated company using an affected version update, and please reply if you have any specific issues.

Body:
□ Overview
o HPE announces security update that addresses vulnerabilities in its products [1]
o Users using the affected version are advised to update to the latest version as per the resolution.

□ Description
o Vulnerabilities in HPE Integrated Lights-Out 5 (iLO 5), Integrated Lights-Out 6 (iLO 6)(CVE-2026-65204) [1]
o Vulnerabilities in HPE Integrated Lights-Out 5 (iLO 5), Integrated Lights-Out 6 (iLO 6), and Integrated Lights-Out 7 (iLO 7)(CVE-2026-65205) [1]
o Vulnerabilities in HPE Integrated Lights-Out 5 (iLO 5), Integrated Lights-Out 6 (iLO 6), and Integrated Lights-Out 7 (iLO 7)(CVE-2026-65206) [1]
o Vulnerabilities in HPE Integrated Lights-Out 5 (iLO 5), Integrated Lights-Out 6 (iLO 6), and Integrated Lights-Out 7 (iLO 7)(CVE-2026-65207) [1]
o Vulnerabilities in HPE Integrated Lights-Out 5 (iLO 5), Integrated Lights-Out 6 (iLO 6), and Integrated Lights-Out 7 (iLO 7)(CVE-2026-65209) [1]
